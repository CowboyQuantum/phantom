    0024 — The consumer contract: one import surface above compile, a session with a forward clock, truth apart from what a user may see, a contract test consumers run, a checked surface snapshot, and 0.x releases

    Status: Accepted Date: 2026-10-27 Step: 8

    Context

    ADR 0003 promised consumers plan §4 (compile, Device.at, Device.measure, Device.truth, optimum), a contract test the qdt adapter also runs, and a changelog. Step 7 built the compiled Device and optimum but no runtime surface with time: runtime_sensed() hands out the migrated qdt-sim core, which a consumer would have to drive, seed and clock itself. qdt's Analysis 0007 (issue #40) found six gaps between the planned surface and what its adapter needs: the sensor bias in sense, a noise seed apart from the device seed, holding voltages without reading, a statement of what one sample is, truth under the names a tune-up recovers, and the line between knowable fields and truth. Rule 2 said nothing imports compile, yet a consumer's one import has to reach compile_device.

    Decision

    • One import surface. phantom.api re-exports every name a consumer uses (compile_device, CompileParams, Device, State, Session, Measurement, SpinOutcome, Truth, SensorTruth, Knowable, optimum and its parts, the layout types, ValleyParams, SpinParams, DEVICE_FORMAT_VERSION, __version__) and adds model_versions(). phantom.api and phantom.contract sit above compile: they may import every layer, contract imports api, and no layer or root module imports either (rule 2 amended; scripts/check_imports.py).
    • The runtime surface lives in device (device.session, device.truth), so it reads the record and the layers below and never compile. Device.at(v, v_sd) is the noise-free State, vectorised. Device.session(noise, dynamics, charge, noise_seed) is a Session with a clock that only moves forward: sense(v, dwell_s, samples, v_sd) reads each point at the end of each dwell, sample k at t₀ + (k+1)·dwell; hold(v, duration_s) keeps the gates at v and evolves the charge there; wait(duration_s) holds at the last point; spin_readout samples the PSB readout of a prepared pair as a protocol step (voltage-driven spin is #41). Device.measure is a fresh session's sense.
    • What one sample is, fixed per session: charge="sampled" (default) puts one charge state in the sensor per sample, the state of a Markov jump trajectory of the rate equation (from the present state, the next is drawn from the column of exp(W·dwell)) with dynamics=True, an independent Boltzmann draw (qdt-sim's behaviour) without; charge="mean" puts ⟨N⟩ in, propagated or thermal. The noise processes are sampled at each sample's time; the white floor keeps the draw's density at 1 ms (σ ∝ √(1 ms / dwell)). A hold samples the gate noise once at its start.
    • Seeds. The device seed is the device's identity. noise_seed=None replays the device's own noise realisation, so identical calls on two fresh sessions are identical; an integer replaces only the noise draw's realisation seed (a hash of both seeds) and keys the session's charge and spin streams, keeping every noise parameter.
    • Knowable and truth. Device.knowable() is what a user may be shown: material, gate names, layout, temperature, programmed point, sensor, barrier and reservoir gates. Everything else on a Device is truth, including the seed and operating_point_v (it holds the compiled sensor set point). Device.truth() gathers it under the quantities a tune-up recovers: the charge lattice in electrons per programmed volt (through the wiring matrix) and the sensor peak in programmed sensor-gate volts (peak, period, charging energy, lever arm, width, the wing at atanh(1/√3) widths and the linear- response sensitivity there at a given bias), so qdt's adapter only renames.
    • Contract test. phantom.contract.run(device) checks every promise above on a device; python -m phantom.contract [device.json ...] runs it on the reference device and on each file, exit status 0 or 1. phantom's CI runs it (tests/test_contract.py); qdt's CI runs it on the pinned tag and its committed device files.
    • Surface snapshot. scripts/api_surface.py writes docs/api/surface.json (every public name, its signature, dataclass fields, public methods); check_all.sh fails when the surface differs from it, so an API change is a reviewed diff with a CHANGELOG.md entry.
    • Releases. Semantic versioning in 0.x: the minor version rises for any breaking change to the surface or to DEVICE_FORMAT_VERSION, the patch version otherwise; a change of output for the same parameters, seed and calls bumps the module's MODEL_VERSION and is listed in the changelog. The owner tags releases (v0.2.0 first); how often phantom releases and qdt moves its pin is the owner's open decision (Step 8).

    Consequences

    • Easier: qdt's adapter becomes renaming and plumbing over Session (its hold/sense protocol maps one to one); a consumer gets a new noise realisation on the same committed device; truth and the knowable fields are stated by phantom, not re-derived by a consumer; an API change cannot slip in unreviewed.
    • Harder: every name in phantom.api is a promise; a session with dynamics costs about 1 ms per sample (one matrix exponential), so long maps belong in dynamics=False or charge="mean"; a point evaluated alone and inside a batch agree within 1e-12 relative, not bit for bit (numpy's kernels).
    • Must now: the owner tags v0.2.0 after this merges; the qdt side (#140, #141, #142) pins it; the compiled rates are constant at runtime (State says so) until a later step makes them voltage- dependent; the wiring draw's sensor-coupling asymmetry is not yet applied by runtime_sensor.

    Alternatives considered

    • Device methods only, no phantom.api — rejected: compile_device lives above device, so a consumer would import from three layers and the surface would have no single place to snapshot.
    • sense returns the equilibrium average only — rejected: qdt samples a charge state per shot, and a trace at a degeneracy must switch; the average stays available as charge="mean".
    • A noise seed that redraws the noise parameters — rejected: the amplitudes and switchers are the device's draw (ADR 0019); only the realisation belongs to a run.
    • Sampling the charge per sample from the propagated distribution — rejected: it loses the correlation between consecutive samples that a slow lead produces; the trajectory keeps it.
    • wait(duration_s, v) instead of hold(v, duration_s) — hold names the gate point first like sense, and wait stays the hold at the last point.
