    0015 — Disorder generators: interface charges as image-charge energy offsets, lithography faults as polygon transforms, one seeded scenario draw

    Status: Accepted Date: 2026-10-26 Step: 3

    Context

    Step 3 turns catalog entries into generators that perturb the device for physical reasons. The catalog entries themselves (#1–#14) are the owner's to write, and only the draft interface-fixed-charge exists, with placeholder densities. The generators must be buildable now, on parameters, and must fit the closed-form electrostatics of Step 2 (ADR 0013) without a solver.

    Decision

    • Interface charges (disorder.charges): a Poisson number of point charges ±e at the interface depth over the layout's bounding box, density × severity. Each adds to the electron energy in the electron plane the potential of the charge plus its image in the pinned surface (the same half-space model as the gate potentials), with a smoothing length for the finite thickness of the electron layer so that charges in the plane itself (MOS) stay finite. No screening by the electrons; the perturbation is an upper bound and the entry is calibration: none until a confirmed reference sets density and sign.
    • Gate misalignment and width error (disorder.geometry_faults): a common shift of the gate layer and an independent outward edge displacement per gate, applied to the polygons. The perturbed lever-arm basis follows from the electrostatics with no new physics.
    • The landscape carries disorder as an offset: GateBasis.offset_ev is a gate-independent energy added to the electron energy; everything above (dot finding, derived CI parameters, sensor coupling) works unchanged on a perturbed basis.
    • One scenario draw (disorder.scenario): material-class parameters + seed + severity → all generators from one generator stream, in a fixed order; to_dict()/from_dict() make the draw plain data a consumer can store. Severity 0 draws nothing and returns the clean layout and basis, bit for bit (P0 test).
    • Constants live in phantom.constants, below every layer, so disorder imports nothing from charge (rule 2).
    • Protocols: the landscape and the coarse stability map, clean beside three draws; and the mean number of unintentional minima against charge density over many draws, which the Step 3 P1 row will compare with a published MOS/SiGe comparison.

    Consequences

    • Easier: disorder is a few hundred microseconds of compile-tier work per draw; every Step 1 and Step 2 protocol can be rendered for a draw by passing a perturbed basis.
    • Harder: without screening, charge densities at the MOS placeholder give large perturbations; the numbers are placeholders until the catalog is written, and nothing here claims a rate.
    • Must now: strain and g-factor draws wait for the spin layer; switchers and drift remain noise processes in readout (Step 4 ties their rates to the catalog); the catalog entry gains calibration: none and tier: runtime.

    Alternatives considered

    • Random potential fields with a chosen correlation length — rejected: not traceable to a catalog quantity.
    • Screened (Thomas–Fermi) charge potentials — deferred to Step 7's solver tier (ADR 0001).
