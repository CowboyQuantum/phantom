    Done this session

    • #53 (ADR 0027): the qubit dots' rates follow the gates.
    • Each CompiledBarrier carries its attempt rate, a tunnelling level held fixed, and the slopes of ln Γ against every gate.
    • At runtime, readout.rates.GateRates and saturating_rates_hz (shared with the sensor's leads) give the rates, and RateEquation.gate_rates uses them.
    • State's three rates are arrays. DEVICE_FORMAT_VERSION 3.
    • New protocol session-closing-barrier. With B_L 0.1 V lower, Γ_L is about 10⁴ Hz, a sweep at 3 µs per point opens a 3.5 mV loop, and a parked session telegraphs.
    • Tests: tests/test_dot_rates.py, 9 P0. Merged as #55.
    • #54 (ADR 0028): the sensor dot's charge is a process in time.
    • A birth–death chain over the sensor's levels with broadened occupations; a sample reads the electrons counted to the drain (exact trajectories at ≤ 200 jumps, otherwise Gaussian with the full-counting-statistics variance; the relaxed closed form beyond 50 relaxation times).
    • Back-action −β_i·(M − M̄), against the sensor's stationary mean.
    • New: readout.sensor_dynamics, Measurement.sensor_charge, protocol session-slow-sensor.
    • Tests: tests/test_sensor_dynamics.py, 10 P0. Merged as #56.
    • Release v0.3.0: annotated tag on 6920f19 after CI passed there (Windows gallery comparison included); GitHub Release "phantom 0.3.0" with the changelog entry as its notes, as for v0.2.0. Noted on #53 and #54.
    • Totals: 174 tests; 72 protocols within budget, all unsigned; contract OK.
