    Current step: none in flight. Released: v0.2.0 (on f72daac), v0.3.0 (on 6920f19) and v0.4.0 (on ae8d5db, carrying #58, #59, #60 and #66). Branch: issue-66/release-v0.4.0 (docs only: the changelog heading and this file). main is at ae8d5db, the merge of #67. Last session: 2026-10-31. The owner asked for the barrier gates to set the interdot coupling, the dots' shape and the exchange (#58–#60). They then found the tune-up behaviour they wanted still missing: the interdot width (anti-detuning) should grow with B_M until the dots form a superdot, and shrink to a grid as it closes. Decisions taken that day: - t in the signal: hybridize the charge states, in the statics and the dynamics. - Dot shape: a grid compiled along the barrier gates and interpolated at runtime. - Splitting: conductance-driven, as an effective interdot capacitance, with a smooth interpolation between Golden & Halperin's limits. - Release: all of it folded into 0.4.0. - Tag: at the end of the day the owner asked for v0.4.0 and a GitHub Release made as for the earlier versions.

    Known gaps

    • Owner-dependent, never blocking: sign-offs for all 76 protocols; catalog entries #1–#14; confirming the thirteen proposed reference entries.
    • Splitting (ADR 0032):
    • A closed B_M stops at the geometric f_C ≈ 0.14 (small hexagons, not a grid).
    • f rises within about 40 mV of B_M.
    • f(g) between g = 0.15 and 0.85 is an interpolation.
    • The metallic-dot theory is applied on top of the single-level hybridization.
    • Exchange (ADR 0031): J jumps where the ground charge state changes; spinless, one valley; the spin layer's Hubbard models keep their few-electron J.
    • Shape (ADR 0030): the gates' changes add; C_m comes from the sphere formula; about 1 % pixel noise; β, the attempt rates and the plungers are not on the grid.
    • Hybridization (ADR 0029): the projection assumes a dwell long against ħ/Ω, with no charge dephasing; spinless.
    • Rates and t: linear in the non-barrier gates; no screening until the FEM backend exists.
    • Sensor process: first-order split; sequential noise; no joint dot–sensor energy (ADR 0028).
    • Placeholders (calibration: none): as listed in the step files and ADRs 0019–0032.
    • Cross-platform (ADR 0014): condition diagonalisations; sum charges relative to a centre; keep window truncation far below 1e-9 at rint ties; propagate generators with non-negative steps; form gaps from N − n_g.
