    Done this session

    • #58 (ADR 0029, merged as #61): the interdot coupling follows every gate and hybridizes the charge states. See CHANGELOG.md.
    • #59 (ADR 0030): the barrier gates reshape the dots.
    • Compile. The double dot is re-derived on 17 nodes along each of B_L, B_M and B_R (±0.2 V). Minima are refined between grid points (refined_dot), and the capacitance helper is capacitance_matrix_af. Each grid ends where the potential stops forming two dots, and that end is marked merged. charge.shape.ShapeGrid; Device.shape; device format 4.
    • Runtime. PCHIP per gate, with the changes added and held beyond each end. ConstantInteractionParams.shape gives C(V) and n_g = C(V)(α(V)·V − Δo(V))/E. InterdotCoupling and GateRates take their exponents along the barrier gates from the grid. Without a shape, everything is 0.3.0's.
    • State gains dot_positions_nm, dot_radii_nm, addition_energies_ev and dots_merged. optimum.coupling inverts t along B_M by Brent's method.
    • On the reference device, B_M +0.15 V draws the dots 18 nm together, C_m grows by 40 % and dot 1's addition energy falls by 1 meV. The dots merge at about +0.13 V from the tuned point.
    • Tests now follow one transition by its electron count, because barrier gates move the plunger lines (absolute charge). The isolated-dot configuration is B_L −0.11 and B_M −0.05.
    • New protocol dot-shape-vs-barrier. Tests: tests/test_dot_shape.py, 9 P0.
    • The hybridized window (amends ADR 0029). The first Windows comparison of this PR differed by 1.8·10⁻⁵ at the middle of an interdot segment. There rint(n_g) ties, and the ±2 chains' truncation left a seam at t ≈ 1 meV. The statics now use ±4, and the dynamics a window chosen by t/E_add (dynamics_window). A 10⁻¹⁵ perturbation now moves interdot-line-vs-barrier by 3·10⁻¹¹, and a P0 test holds the seam below 10⁻¹⁰.
    • The session's propagator. session-closing-barrier then differed by 1.1·10⁻⁹ on Windows. A latched slow transition is read through entries of exp(W·dt) that expm keeps only to about ε‖W·dt‖ in absolute terms. propagator keeps every entry's relative accuracy (non-negative Taylor steps and squarings). State also keeps its last diagonalisation, which cuts that protocol's compile phase from 1.0 s to 0.6 s.

    Known gaps

    • Owner-dependent, never blocking: sign-offs for all 74 protocols; catalog entries #1–#14; confirming the ten proposed reference entries.
    • Shape (ADR 0030):
    • The gates' changes add, with no cross terms.
    • C_m comes from the sphere formula, so a merged double dot never becomes one dot; points past the merge are held and flagged.
    • The compile tier's curvature has pixel noise of about 1 %.
    • The sensor's β and the attempt rates stay at the operating point. The plungers are not on the grid.
    • A rate along a barrier gate levels off at about half the attempt rate.
    • Hybridization (ADR 0029):
    • The projection between samples assumes a dwell long against ħ/Ω. There is no charge dephasing and no Landau–Zener step.
    • The model is spinless.
    • State.exchange_ev still uses the barrier-only exponential, until #60.
    • Rates and t: linear in the gates other than the barrier gates, within about 20 % over ±10 mV. No screening until the FEM backend exists.
    • Sensor process: first-order split within a dwell; sequential noise; no joint dot–sensor energy (ADR 0028).
    • Placeholders (calibration: none): as listed in the step files and ADRs 0019–0030.
    • Cross-platform (ADR 0014): avoid digit-cancelling quantities. Condition diagonalisations and sum charges relative to a centre.
