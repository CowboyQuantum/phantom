    0019 — Noise is drawn per device: a 1/f amplitude, a Poisson number of dominant switchers, a white floor

    Status: Accepted Date: 2026-10-26 Step: 4

    Context

    Step 1 typed one set of noise processes for every device (150 µV rms 1/f, one 4 mV switcher at 0.5 Hz, 15 pA white). A phantom instance should carry its own noise as a draw (ADR 0002): devices differ in their 1/f amplitude by a factor of a few, in how many dominant two-level fluctuators sit near the sensor, and in those fluctuators' rates over decades. The catalog entries that will set these distributions (#1 oxide traps and fluctuators, #12 slow drift) are not written yet.

    Decision

    • disorder.noise_draw draws, per device and seed: the 1/f amplitude (log-normal around a material median), a Poisson number of dominant switchers with log-normal amplitudes and log-uniform up and down rates, and the white floor. Severity scales the amplitude and the switcher count; severity 0 leaves only the white floor. The draw is part of the scenario draw and serialises with it.
    • readout.SensedDoubleDot.from_noise_draw turns a draw into processes: the 1/f ensemble on the gates, the switchers summed at the sensor, the white floor rescaled to the sampling interval. readout imports disorder (downward); disorder holds only numbers.
    • The 1/f band reaches down to 0.1 mHz so that cuts repeated over hours drift (drift-repeated-cuts); the dominant switcher's dwell-time statistics are the switcher-histogram protocol.
    • plunger-vs-barrier-map (Step 1) is re-rendered on the master equation with WKB lead rates per barrier row (ADR 0017). The lock-in dynamics stay on the Step 1 rate-landscape placeholder for now: one master-equation step per half-cycle would cost minutes per map; this is recorded as a gap.
    • All numbers are placeholders (calibration: none) until the catalog entries and the charge-noise reference are confirmed; the Step 4 P1 row on the charge-noise spectrum then fixes the median.

    Consequences

    • Easier: noise realism is a draw like disorder; the SNR and histogram protocols vary by device.
    • Harder: one more set of placeholders awaiting the owner; the lock-in placeholder lingers.
    • Must now: readout.loading keeps LeadRateLandscape and latched_occupation for the lock-in only, and says so; LeadBarrier and the old plunger-vs-barrier path are gone.

    Alternatives considered

    • One typed noise set per material — rejected: ADR 0002.
    • A physical trap ensemble with capture/emission energetics — deferred to when entry #1 exists.
