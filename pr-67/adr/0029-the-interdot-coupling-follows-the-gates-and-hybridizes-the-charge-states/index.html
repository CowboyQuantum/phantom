    0029 — The interdot coupling follows the gates and hybridizes the charge states: t from half the slopes of ln Γ_m, capped at ħω₀/π; eigenstates of the CI window in the statics and a Pauli master equation over them in the dynamics

    Status: Accepted Date: 2026-10-31 Step: 8 follow-up (issue #58, the first of #58–#60; after ADR 0027)

    Context

    In a simulated tune-up the owner found that "the barrier gates do not actually modulate the interdot coupling AT ALL" (2026-10-31). On 0.3.0:

    • t on paper only. State.tunnel_coupling_ev was t₀·exp((V_BM − V₀)/v_scale): it followed the barrier gate alone, had no ceiling (15 meV at B_M + 50 mV on the reference device) and entered nothing a sensor reads. It reached only the exchange and the transport placeholders.
    • A classical charge. The sensor read the classical CI model and its rate equation. Opening B_M did not change an interdot line, apart from the interdot rate of ADR 0027 and a fixed cross-capacitance.
    • The owner's decisions (2026-10-31). Hybridize the charge states in the statics and the dynamics. Reshape the dots with a compiled grid in #59. Follow with the exchange in #60. Release all three as 0.4.0.

    Four forces shaped the design:

    • Cost. State is evaluated on grids of 10⁴–10⁵ points, and a session pays one matrix exponential per sample. A per-point diagonalisation must stay at microseconds.
    • Consistency. ADR 0027 already compiles the slopes of ln Γ_m against every gate at a fixed tunnelling level. Γ_m ∝ T and t ∝ √T come from the same cut.
    • Limits. At t → 0 the result must be the CI model, which the P0 tests and every earlier protocol rely on.
    • Reproducibility (ADR 0014). Eigenvalues near 0.05 eV with kT = 13 µeV lose digits in a Boltzmann weight unless the matrix is conditioned.

    Decision

    The interdot coupling follows every gate, and the CI window's charge states of one total charge hybridize through it. The statics use the window's eigenstates, and the dynamics are a Pauli master equation over them. Specifically:

    • t at the gates. t(V) = t_max·σ(ln(t_ref/(t_max − t_ref)) + ½ s_m·ΔV):
    • t_ref is the compiled t at the operating point.
    • s_m are the slopes of ln Γ_m of ADR 0027.
    • t_max = ħω₀/π is compile's t = ħω₀√T/π at a transmission of one.
    • The form is saturating_rates_hz's. charge.hybrid.InterdotCoupling carries it, and Device.runtime_coupling() builds it from fields the device file already has, so the device format is unchanged.
    • Hybridization.
    • H_N = Σ U(N)|N⟩⟨N| − t Σ(|N₁+1, N₂−1⟩⟨N₁, N₂| + h.c.) on the window's states of each total charge, spinless with one level per dot.
    • The window is the CI window's (2w+1)² grid with its centre held at or above zero, and a negative occupation left out.
    • Each block is a chain of at most 2w+1 states, diagonalised in batches with its lowest level taken out first, so the eigenvalues keep their digits.
    • Charges are summed relative to the window's centre.
    • Statics. With a coupling, ConstantInteractionParams gives the occupation as the diagonal of the thermal density matrix of the eigenstates. ⟨N⟩, the sensor's potential and current, and the lock-in follow from it. The ground state is the dominant charge state of the lowest eigenstate. Without a coupling the model is unchanged, bit for bit.
    • Dynamics. RateEquation.eigen_rate_matrix gives the rates between eigenstates:
    • Lead i adds an electron from k to l at Γ_i·|⟨l|c†_i|k⟩|²·f((E_l − E_k)/kT) and removes it at the complement.
    • Within a total charge, k relaxes to l at Γ_m·g_kl·f(·), with g_kl = Σ_b w_bk·w_bl the product of the two eigenstates' weights on each bond b between charge states one moved electron apart. That is the sum of |⟨l|X_b|k⟩|² + |⟨l|Z_b|k⟩|², the bond's transfer and its dipole.
    • g is exactly Γ_m's classical rule at t = 0, and Γ_m for an isolated pair at every detuning.
    • Detailed balance holds in the eigenenergies.
    • When the gates move, the distribution is projected onto the new eigenstates, q_l = Σ_k p_k|⟨l|k⟩|², with coherences dropped. sweep and Session share this.
    • What a sample reads. A sampled session draws an eigenstate, and the sensor reads its ⟨N⟩. Its total charge is an integer and its split need not be. Measurement.n_present is that charge, and the contract checks the total. State.tunnel_coupling_ev is the gated t.
    • Release. 0.4.0 (breaking). Model versions: charge.hybrid 1, readout.master_equation 2, device 5, device.session 5, optimum.targets 2.

    Consequences

    • Easier:
    • Opening B_M widens an interdot line from the thermal step to ½(1 − ε/Ω·tanh(Ω/2kT)). On the reference device the 10–90 % width goes from 0.22 mV to 0.65 mV as t goes from 0.05 to 30 µeV (interdot-line-vs-barrier).
    • Closing B_M is what isolates a dot. With B_L closed alone, dot 2's lead loads dot 1 at Γ_R·t²(1/Δ + 1/Δ')². The electron-like and hole-like paths add, as in elastic cotunnelling, at about 10⁶ Hz against Γ_L ≈ 10⁴ Hz. With both closed, a sweep latches and a parked point telegraphs (session-closing-barrier).
    • optimum(device).coupling inverts the same t a session uses.
    • Harder:
    • Validity of the projection. The projection assumes a dwell long against ħ/Ω and dephasing in the eigenbasis. At |ε| ≲ t it shares the electron between the dots even when t is so small that the coherent transfer would take longer than the dwell (t ≲ ħ/dwell, sub-neV at microsecond dwells). On the reference device t stays above 0.05 µeV, and a sweep lands that close to a degeneracy rarely. There is no charge-dephasing rate and no Landau–Zener step.
    • Plunger sensitivity. t inherits the unscreened sensitivity of #48. The plungers move it as much as B_M, and two cells 18 mV apart on B_M differ by a factor of 30 in t at their interdot lines.
    • Fixed shape and spin. The dots' capacitances, lever arms and positions stay those of the operating point until #59. The model is spinless, so there is no √2 on singlet tunnelling (that is #60's sector) and no valley structure.
    • Cost. A grid point costs about 7 µs instead of 1.3 µs in the statics, and a sample a few small diagonalisations more.
    • Exchange. J still comes from the Hubbard model with the barrier-only exponential, so State.exchange_ev and State.tunnel_coupling_ev disagree until #60.
    • Must now:
    • A consumer that labels charge states from n_present rounds it. An interdot line is no longer a step.
    • A test that isolates a dot closes its own barrier and B_M.
    • The contract's sample_semantics and hold_moves_the_charge checks compare the total charge.

    Alternatives considered

    • Hybridizing statics only. Rejected by the owner. A session's charge would disagree with State near every interdot line.
    • A separate slope compile for t (central differences of interdot_tunnel_coupling_ev per gate). Rejected. It costs 24 more barrier actions per compile, its level moves with the gates (the rule ADR 0027 rejected), and ADR 0027 already shows the two agree on B_M.
    • Full density-matrix (Bloch–Redfield) dynamics. Rejected for cost: 25² states per sample. A charge-dephasing rate and a Landau–Zener step would lift the projection's limit, and are left for when a protocol needs them.
    • Incoherent resonant tunnelling between localized states (a Lorentzian Γ(ε)). Rejected. It cannot give the polarization width √(ε² + 4t²) at t ≫ kT, which is what a tune-up reads.
    • One 25×25 diagonalisation per point. Rejected. Different total charges are degenerate across blocks, so the eigenvectors could mix them, and it costs more.
    • An integer n_present (the eigenstate's dominant charge state) beside a non-integer sensor input. Rejected: n_present would no longer be what entered the sensor.
