    The consumer contract

    What phantom promises a consumer (ADR 0003, 0024). Everything here is reachable from one import, phantom.api, is recorded in docs/api/surface.json, and is checked by python -m phantom.contract, which a consumer runs against the release it pins and against its own device files.

    from phantom.api import CompileParams, Device, compile_device, optimum, two_dot_layout

op = {"B_L": 0.15, "P1": 0.6, "B_M": 0.2, "P2": 0.6, "B_R": 0.15, "SG": 0.6, "R_L": 0.7, "R_R": 0.7,
      "SB_L": 0.4, "SB_R": 0.3, "SR_L": 0.7, "SR_R": 0.7}
device = compile_device(two_dot_layout("SiGe", 80.0), CompileParams(operating_point_v=op), seed=2)
device.to_json("device.json")             # canonical JSON, about 13 KB; Device.from_json reloads it

run = device.session(noise_seed=7)        # one run: a clock, the device's noise, the dot charge
run.hold(v_pulse, 1e-6)                   # sit at v_pulse for 1 µs; the charge evolves there
m = run.sense(v_read, dwell_s=1e-3, samples=100, v_sd=50e-6)
m.times_s, m.current_a, m.n_present       # (100,), (100,), (100, 2)

    The surface

    Call Returns Notes
    compile_device(layout, CompileParams, seed) Device deterministic in the seed; the slow tier (about 0.15 s)
    Device.to_json, dumps, from_json, loads text-identical round trip; DEVICE_FORMAT_VERSION
    Device.at(v, v_sd=None) State noise-free, no time; vectorised over leading axes of v (…, gates)
    Device.session(noise=True, dynamics=True, charge="sampled", noise_seed=None) Session one run
    Session.sense(v, dwell_s, samples=None, v_sd=None) Measurement reads; advances the clock
    Session.hold(v, duration_s) no read; advances the clock; the charge evolves at v
    Session.wait(duration_s) hold at the last point given
    Session.spin_readout(p_triplet, detuning_ev, shots, t_integration_s, t1_read_s) SpinOutcome PSB readout of a prepared pair
    Session.time_s, Session.last_point_v the clock (s); the last programmed point
    Device.measure(v, dwell_s, samples, v_sd, noise, dynamics, charge, noise_seed) Measurement a fresh session's sense
    Device.knowable() Knowable what a user may be shown
    Device.truth(lattice_gates, near_v, v_sd, v_programmed) Truth hidden parameters
    optimum(device, t_target_ev) Optimum the best achievable: sensor, lattice, coupling, readout, coherence
    model_versions() dict[str, int] every module's MODEL_VERSION, for a consumer's provenance

    Voltages are always the programmed gate voltages, one per entry of Device.gate_names, in volts. phantom maps them through the device's wiring draw (open and shorted gates) to the voltages the gates carry; State.v_carried_v shows the result.

    Time

    A session's clock starts at zero and only moves forward. sense holds each point for samples dwells (one when samples is None) and reads it at the end of each: sample k of a call is read at t₀ + (k + 1)·dwell, t₀ the clock when the call starts, and the clock ends at the last sample. hold and wait advance it by their duration and read nothing; spin_readout advances it by shots × t_integration_s. A zero or negative dwell, or a negative hold, raises ValueError. Before its first call the device has sat at the first point long enough to be in equilibrium there.

    What one sample is

    At each sample's time the noise processes of the device's draw are sampled: a common-mode gate offset added to the carried voltages, the switchers near the sensor shifting its potential, and white readout noise added to the current. The white floor keeps its density: σ is the draw's value at 1 ms times √(1 ms / dwell). The charge in the sensor depends on two settings, fixed per session:

    dynamics=True (default) dynamics=False
    charge="sampled" (default) one state of a Markov jump trajectory of the rate equation: from the state present, the next is drawn from exp(W·dwell); consecutive samples are correlated when the rates are slow against the dwell an independent Boltzmann draw at each sample (qdt-sim's behaviour)
    charge="mean" ⟨N⟩ of the distribution propagated by exp(W·dwell): latching and lag the thermal ⟨N⟩; without noise this is State.sensor_current_a exactly

    Measurement.n_present is the charge that entered the sensor. In sampled mode it is one eigenstate of the dots' charge (ADR 0029): its total charge is an integer, and its split between the dots is the eigenstate's ⟨N⟩, an integer up to the interdot admixture (t/Δ)² away from interdot lines and near half an electron each way at the middle of one when t > kT. In mean mode it is ⟨N⟩. A hold samples the gate noise once at its start and keeps it through the hold. With dynamics a sample costs one matrix exponential, about 1 ms; without, about 0.05 ms.

    Seeds and reproducibility

    • The device seed is the device's identity: it fixes the disorder, wiring, valley, spin and noise draws. A device file pins all of them even when a later phantom compiles differently.
    • noise_seed=None replays the device's own noise realisation, so two fresh sessions given identical calls return identical results. An integer noise_seed keeps every noise parameter of the draw and gives a new realisation; the same integer replays it.
    • Identical calls give identical output bit for bit on the same platform and numpy build, and agree within 1e-9 relative across platforms (ADR 0010, 0014). A point evaluated alone and the same point inside a batch agree within 1e-12 relative, not bit for bit.

    Knowable and truth

    Device.knowable() holds what a person running the device knows before measuring: the material, the gate names, the layout, the electron temperature, the programmed point, the sensor, barrier and reservoir gates, and the sensor's own barrier and bath gates (sensor_barrier_gates, sensor_reservoir_gates, empty for a layout without them). Everything else on a Device is truth, including the seed (recompiling from it reproduces every draw), params, the scenario draw and operating_point_v, which holds the carried voltages and the sensor set point compilation chose. A consumer that hides truth from a user forwards knowable() and measurements, never the Device, truth() or optimum(). phantom has no hidden mode.

    Device.truth() gathers the hidden parameters, and states two quantities a tune-up recovers:

    • charge_lattice_e_per_v: electrons per programmed volt on (dot 1, dot 2) [rows] from the two lattice_gates [columns], through the wiring draw.
    • sensor (SensorTruth, None when the sensor gate does not move the sensor): with the other gates at v_programmed (the programmed point by default) and the dots' charge held at its thermal value there, the peak in programmed sensor-gate volts nearest near_v, the period, the sensor's charging energy, its lever arm (charging energy / period), the thermal width w of I ∝ sech²((V − V_peak)/w), the sensor's two tunnel rates at that peak and their lifetime width ħ(Γ_S + Γ_D) in sensor-gate volts, the wing (the steepest point of the linear-response peak above it) and the sensitivity |dI/dV| there at bias v_sd. The peak is the thermal sech² convolved with a Lorentzian of the lifetime width (ADR 0026); with a negligible lifetime width the wing is V_peak + w·atanh(1/√3) and the sensitivity 4h/(3√3 w), h the linear-response peak height. The rates are those at the peak: along the sensor gate they change by a factor e every few mV, so the truth states each peak with its own rates.

    The sensor of a compiled device is a dot with two barriers and a bath behind each (ADR 0026). Its peaks are a few hundred microvolts wide and tens of millivolts apart on the sensor gate, so the programmed sensor gate alone usually sits between peaks: tune it first, as on a device (the compiled set point in operating_point_v, the truth's peak and wing, and optimum(device).sensor are where a tune-up lands). State.sensor_gamma_hz gives its source and drain rates at any point: they follow the sensor's barrier, bath and plunger gates and every other gate's cross-talk, and a bath gate below its turn-on empties that side.

    The dots' own rates follow the gates too (ADR 0027): State.gamma_left_hz, gamma_right_hz and gamma_interdot_hz are arrays with the input's leading axes, the compiled rates moved by their slopes against every gate and capped at the attempt rate. A session's dynamics use the rates at each sample's voltages: closing a barrier gate slows its lead until a sweep latches and a parked point telegraphs. Truth states the rates at the operating point.

    The interdot tunnel coupling follows every gate as well (ADR 0029): State.tunnel_coupling_ev is the compiled t moved by half the slopes of ln Γ_m and capped at ħω₀/π, and the charge states of one total charge hybridize through it. An interdot line is then the two-level polarization ½(1 − ε/Ω·tanh(Ω/2kT)), Ω = √(ε² + 4t²), so opening the barrier gate widens it; State.n_mean and the sensor read the eigenstates, and a session's dynamics run over them. A dot is isolated only when its own barrier and the interdot barrier are both closed: with B_M open, dot 2's lead loads dot 1 in second order in t. optimum(device).coupling inverts this t along the barrier gate.

    With dynamics, the sensor dot's own charge is a process on the same clock (ADR 0028). Each sample's current is the charge that left to the drain during the dwell over the dwell, so a fast sensor reads its stationary current with shot noise shrinking as 1/√dwell, and a sensor slowed by its barriers or baths reads single-electron bursts while its charge switches. Measurement.sensor_charge holds that charge at the end of each dwell (only its changes are physical). The sensor acts back: its charge relative to its stationary mean shifts each dot's levels by −β_i per electron. Without dynamics nothing of this applies, and a long mean-mode dwell still reads State.sensor_current_a (within 1e-6).

    Versions and releases

    • phantom.__version__ follows semantic versioning in 0.x: the minor version rises for any breaking change to this surface or to DEVICE_FORMAT_VERSION, the patch version otherwise. Every change to the surface shows in docs/api/surface.json and is listed in CHANGELOG.md.
    • A change of output for the same parameters, seed and calls bumps the MODEL_VERSION of the module that changed. Device.provenance records the compile-time versions; model_versions() gives them all.
    • A release is an annotated tag vX.Y.Z on a green main commit, made by the owner or by a session the owner tells to (ADR 0025); a consumer pins a tag. How often phantom releases and consumers move their pins is an open decision (Step 8).

    Running the contract test

    python -m phantom.contract                      # the reference device
python -m phantom.contract devices/*.json       # and every device file a consumer commits

    It prints one line per check and exits 0 when every check holds. The checks: the surface imports; compile is deterministic; the round trip is text-identical and the reload answers bit for bit; provenance names every model version; at shapes, finiteness and bias; knowable fields; truth fields and the sensor's relations; fresh sessions replay, noise seeds replay and differ; the clock; equilibrium; bias in sense; sample semantics; a hold moves the charge; optimum has five finite parts; spin_readout shapes and clock.

    Not yet in the contract

    • Every rate, the dots' and the sensor's, follows the gate voltages through a compiled exponential that holds within about 20 % of the compile tier's change over ±10 mV and drifts beyond (ADR 0026, 0027).
    • Within one dwell the sensor sees the dots' charge at its end and the dots see the sensor's at its start (a first-order splitting, ADR 0028).
    • spin_readout takes a prepared triplet probability; spin driven by gate voltages over time is issue #41.
