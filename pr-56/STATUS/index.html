    Done this session

    • #53 (ADR 0027): the qubit dots' rates follow the gates.
    • Each CompiledBarrier carries its attempt rate, a tunnelling level held fixed, and the slopes of ln Γ against every gate.
    • At runtime, readout.rates.GateRates and saturating_rates_hz (shared with the sensor's leads) give the rates, and RateEquation.gate_rates uses them.
    • State's three rates are arrays. DEVICE_FORMAT_VERSION 3.
    • New protocol session-closing-barrier. With B_L 0.1 V lower, Γ_L is about 10⁴ Hz, a sweep at 3 µs per point opens a 3.5 mV loop, and a parked session telegraphs.
    • session-hold-relaxation now overlays the total rate out of the held state. The interdot path had doubled the relaxation rate before as well.
    • Tests: tests/test_dot_rates.py, 9 P0.
    • #54 (ADR 0028): the sensor dot's charge is a process in time.
    • With dynamics, the sensor's charge is a birth–death chain over its levels with broadened occupations, so its stationary current is exactly ADR 0026's.
    • A sample reads the electrons that left to the drain during its dwell: from exact trajectories at ≤ 200 jumps per dwell, otherwise a Gaussian with the full-counting-statistics variance.
    • Back-action is −β_i·(M − M̄), against the sensor's stationary mean.
    • New code: readout.sensor_dynamics and Measurement.sensor_charge.
    • New protocol session-slow-sensor.
    • Tests: tests/test_sensor_dynamics.py, 10 P0.
