    Reference library (ADR 0007)

    The only admissible standard for a claim that phantom agrees with physics. One entry per paper under entries/<id>.yaml (schema: SCHEMA.md); digitized curves under data/<id>/.

    • A P1 test is @pytest.mark.literature("<id>", item="<label>") and loads its curve from data/.
    • Catalog entries cite ids in sources and rate.
    • A session may add an entry as proposed. Only a human sets confirmed, after opening the paper and checking the DOI, the item and the digitized values. Tests against a proposed entry are skipped.
    • scripts/check_references.py (run by check_all.sh, from Step 1) checks ids, DOIs and markers.
